Client and CA certificates were generated into secret

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "takserver.certsSecretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.admin\.p12}' | base64 -d > admin.p12

  Keystore/truststore password: the value of certs.keystorePassword.

NOTE: the certs Secret is not owned by this release - `helm uninstall` will
not remove it. Delete it manually to regenerate certificates on reinstall.
